Actilo is an activity journal for a small circle of friends. You choose what to log, how your character looks and whether to share optional diagnostics.

Actilo is run by Oleksandr Panarin, an independent developer based in Ukraine. For privacy questions or requests, email fjgames.apps@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Sign-in details. Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication handle your Google account identity, email address, basic profile information and authentication tokens. Actilo uses an account identifier to keep your data separate. We never receive your Google password.
  • Your Actilo profile. Your chosen name, body type, portrait, colors and effects, friendship code, friend list and pending friend requests.
  • Activity you publish. The date, your self-reported muscle and Mind ratings, and your lifetime check-in count. These entries can reveal information about your fitness and wellbeing. They are not measurements from a medical device.
  • Notification details. If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging provides a delivery token. We store that token with a random installation identifier so notifications can reach the right device. We do not collect your phone's contacts to find friends.
  • Optional diagnostics. If you enable usage analytics, Google Analytics for Firebase processes information such as app sessions, approximate country, app version and device information. If you enable crash reporting, Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports, stack traces and technical device information. Both choices start off. Our app does not add your name, email, activity ratings or purchase proof to analytics events or attach a user identifier to crash reports. Custom screen and action tracking is disabled.
  • Purchases. Google Play handles payment. Actilo receives purchase details and signed purchase proof to check and restore the Supporter Pack. It may provide Play with an obfuscated account identifier for fraud prevention. We do not receive your card number or banking details.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. Please do not send passwords, sign-in codes or full purchase tokens.

What stays on your phone

Draft check-ins, reminder times, chosen reminder days, quiet hours and diagnostic preferences are stored locally. A draft is not shared with friends. Publishing creates a cloud upload; if you are offline, the upload waits on your phone.

The app also keeps a local copy of your profile, calendar and friend information, along with changes waiting to sync. Changes to your appearance may stay local until the next sync. Information displayed about friends can be out of date while you are offline.

Test builds may include a local request counter. It stores dates and aggregate counts of Worker API requests, not request bodies, names, account IDs or tokens. It is shared by accounts using that installation, survives sign-out and is not uploaded to us.

What your friends see

Accepted friends can see your chosen name, character and appearance, lifetime check-in count, and the activity you publish for the current day. There is no private-publication switch. Your earlier calendar entries are not shared with friends.

People involved in a pending friend request can see a basic profile, not your activity ratings. Google sign-in email addresses and device notification tokens are not included in friend cards.

A reaction sends the recipient a notification with your name, the selected emoji and its short message. We keep the sender's last reaction date and the IDs already reacted to that day to prevent repeats. We do not keep a reaction-message history. A new day's first reaction replaces that list.

Why we use this information

We use account and activity data to sign you in, keep your journal across installations, show your chosen friends today's activity, deliver friend notifications and restore purchases. Optional diagnostics help us understand basic usage and fix crashes. Support messages let us answer your requests.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, the core service uses data to provide the features you request. Optional analytics and crash reporting rely on your choice to enable them. Service security, handling support and meeting legal obligations may involve other applicable grounds. Where your activity data requires additional permission under local law, that permission must cover its storage and sharing; publishing is your instruction to share the entry with your accepted friends. You can stop future sharing by not publishing, remove friends or delete your account.

Actilo has no advertising integration. We do not sell your activity data or use your ratings to target ads.

Services that process data

  • Google: Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Messaging, optional Analytics and Crashlytics, and Google Play billing. Support email is handled through Gmail. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.
  • Cloudflare: the Worker API processes authenticated requests, and D1 stores your profile, published check-ins, friend connections and device delivery details. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts these legal pages. Visiting them involves GitHub's normal hosting and request processing. There is no analytics, advertising or third-party font integration on the site. The account-deletion page loads Google Firebase Authentication to let you identify and delete your own account. It uses browser session storage for sign-in and deletion progress, not for advertising. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

These providers can process information outside your home country under their applicable terms and data-protection arrangements. We use HTTPS for the app's cloud requests and verify the account identity before providing access to account data. No internet service can promise that a security incident will never happen.

How long information stays

  • Profile and friendships: kept while your account exists, unless you change or remove them.
  • Activity history: the app syncs the current calendar month and the previous two months. Older cloud entries are cleared when your account next syncs or publishes, rather than by a nightly cleanup. An inactive account may therefore retain older entries until that happens or the account is deleted. Your lifetime check-in count is kept separately until account deletion.
  • Device registrations: replaced when a token changes, removed during normal sign-out when possible, or removed when the provider reports an invalid token. Registrations older than 90 days are eligible for cleanup when that account next performs a calendar sync. Sign-out also attempts to invalidate the token on the device.
  • Local data: remains until the app replaces or removes it, account deletion completes on that installation, or you clear the app's storage. Device backup and restore can affect what is recovered after reinstalling.
  • Diagnostics and support: retained for handling issues and requests, subject to the relevant provider's retention settings and applicable law. Contact us about access or deletion. Turning off diagnostics stops future optional collection; it does not by itself erase information already received by a provider.

Google Play retains its transaction records under its own rules. Removing an Actilo account does not remove those records. Provider backups and security records can have a separate retention cycle; deletion from the active app database is not a promise of instant erasure from every backup.

Your choices and account deletion

You can edit your name and appearance in Settings. You can change optional analytics and crash reporting in Settings → Diagnostics, and manage notification permission in your phone's settings. Quiet hours only affect local reminders, not incoming friend notifications.

To delete your account, use the delete-account button in the Settings header. Google may ask you to confirm your identity. The completed flow removes the Firebase Authentication account, the Actilo cloud profile, published history, device registrations and references to that account in friends' lists. It also clears account data from the installation completing deletion.

To delete without the app, open Delete your account, sign in through Google's window and confirm deletion on the page. Sign-in alone does not delete anything. If you cannot use that process, contact support. We never ask you to email a password or a sign-in code. A failed or interrupted deletion is not treated as completed; follow the page's instructions or contact support.

Other installations may still hold an old offline copy until they reconnect or their local storage is cleared. We cannot recall a notification already displayed or information a friend has independently saved. Deleting Actilo does not delete your Google account, cancel a Google Play transaction or automatically request a refund.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, withdraw consent, object to certain uses, or complain to your local data-protection authority. Email us to make a request. We handle requests under the rules that apply to you.

Younger users

Actilo does not ask for your date of birth or provide a separate parental-consent flow. You must be allowed to use the service and Google sign-in under the rules that apply to you. If you believe a child has provided personal information without required permission, contact us so we can investigate and, where appropriate, remove it.

Updates and contact

We update this page when the app's data practices change and show the new effective date above. If a change requires a new permission, updating this page alone does not replace that permission.
