What Actilo is for

Actilo lets you log movement or meditation, customize a character and share today's check-in with a small circle of friends. Your ratings describe how an activity felt to you. They are not medical measurements, verified performance results or advice about what exercise is safe for you.

There are no rankings or daily streak penalties. Rest days do not erase your lifetime check-in count. Use the app at your own pace. If you need medical or exercise advice, speak to an appropriately qualified professional.

Your account and your friends

Sign in with a Google account you are entitled to use. Keep access to that account secure. You must be allowed to use Actilo under the laws and Google account rules that apply to you, including any required permission from a parent or guardian.

Choose your friends carefully: accepted friends can see your profile, appearance, lifetime check-in count and today's published ratings. Earlier calendar entries are not shared. Publishing locks that day's entry; review it before confirming. A draft stays on your phone until you publish it.

Do not impersonate someone, harass another user, use friend requests or reactions to spam, interfere with the service, access someone else's account or falsify payment proof. You can remove a friend in the app. Contact support if another account is being misused. We may restrict access where needed to address misuse, security issues or legal requirements.

Free features and the Supporter Pack

Core check-ins, Mind ratings, your calendar and friends do not require the Supporter Pack.

Offline use and availability

Actilo keeps a local cache and saves pending changes on your phone. Offline changes reach friends only after a successful sync. Friend status can be out of date, and push delivery depends on network access, Google services and the recipient's phone settings.

Service limits, outages and device problems can interrupt cloud features. The app keeps pending uploads where possible, but clearing app data or losing the device before an upload succeeds can lose information that exists only on that phone. Do not use Actilo as the only record of information you cannot afford to lose.

The app and its visual catalog may change. The Supporter Pack covers the features described at purchase; it does not promise every possible future feature. Changes affecting paid access remain subject to applicable consumer law and store rules.
