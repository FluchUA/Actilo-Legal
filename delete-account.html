You can delete your Actilo account in the app or directly on this website if you no longer have it installed. Signing out or uninstalling the app alone does not delete your cloud account.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Actilo and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings and tap the delete-account icon in the top-right corner.
  3. Read the warning and confirm. Google may ask you to sign in again to verify ownership.

Keep the app connected until deletion finishes. If an error appears, do not assume the account is already gone. Follow the instructions or contact support.

Delete on this website

  1. Use Continue with Google above and choose the Google account you use for Actilo.
  2. Check the signed-in account and read the deletion warning.
  3. Tick the confirmation box, then choose Delete my Actilo account.
  4. Keep the page open until it confirms that account deletion is complete.

The website removes your Actilo cloud data first, then your Firebase sign-in account. Signing in alone does not delete anything. Google may ask you to sign in again if too much time passes before you confirm.

If the second step fails after cloud data has been removed, the page explains what remains and lets you continue. It does not report success until both steps finish. It does not create a new Actilo cloud profile as part of a retry.

If Google sign-in or deletion does not work, email fjgames.apps@gmail.com and describe the problem. Do not send your password, verification codes, card details or full purchase tokens.

What is removed

  • Your Actilo sign-in account in Firebase Authentication.
  • Your Actilo cloud profile, name, body and appearance choices, friendship code and lifetime check-in count.
  • Your published activity history and stored device notification registrations.
  • Friend connections, pending requests and references to your account in other users' friend and reaction-recipient lists.

The in-app flow also clears account data from the installation completing deletion. Website deletion does not remotely wipe local storage on an offline phone. Clear the app's storage on other installations if needed; old cached information may remain there until the app reconnects or that storage is cleared.

What is separate

Deleting Actilo does not delete your Google account. Google Play keeps its own purchase and transaction records. Deletion does not request a refund. The one-time Supporter Pack can be restored with the Play account that bought it, subject to that account's current purchase status.

Previously delivered notifications or copies another person saved cannot be recalled. Optional diagnostic records, support correspondence, provider backups and records required by law may have separate handling or retention. The Privacy Policy explains these limits. Contact us about any related data request.

You can sign in again with the same Google email later. That creates a new Actilo account; your deleted activity and friendships are not restored.
